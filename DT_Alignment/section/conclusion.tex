\section{Conclusion}


Theorem~\ref{thm:property-transfer} states transfer at the syntactic
level of model labels, which matches existing model-checking
workflows. The framework also supports requirements specified
directly over the ontology signature: a TCTL formula whose atomic
propositions are first-order formulas over $(\mathcal{S},
\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{R})$ can be evaluated on $V_P$ and $V_D$ via
the interpretations $I_{V_P}, I_{V_D}$, and aligned views satisfy the
same such formulas without recourse to $\tau$. We leave the formal
development of this extension to future work.
